Privacy, terms & safety

WalkieTalkiePro lets invited people talk in a shared online room. It is not an emergency service or a replacement for a phone, licensed radio, or safe driving practices.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

PrivacyTermsSafety

Privacy

This version does not require an account. It uses Google Analytics to measure site usage, including visits, session activity, approximate location, and browser/device information. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. See how Google uses cookies. The app also uses browser storage for your display name, recent room/session information, alert voice choice, and room theme. This information stays in that browser until its storage is cleared.

Information used in a room

To run a room, the service processes the name you enter, room name and invite code, a temporary connection identity, and room activity such as who is speaking. Quick alerts and acknowledgements include the sender name and time. Anyone in the room can see shared alerts and hear live audio.

Location sharing is off until you turn it on. While enabled, your coordinates are shared with everyone in that room and kept in server memory only. They are removed when you stop sharing or after 90 seconds without an update. The map loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which may receive standard connection data and requests for the map area shown.

Audio and transcripts

When you hold Talk, your live audio is streamed through the app’s self-hosted LiveKit service on its hosting server to other room participants. WalkieTalkiePro does not intentionally record or save call audio.

Where supported by your browser, speech recognition can create live and final transcripts while you talk. Live text is briefly shared with room participants; a completed transcript appears as a room alert for about 60 seconds. Browser speech-recognition services may process audio according to the browser provider’s own settings and privacy terms. Transcript accuracy is not guaranteed.

Storage and service providers

Room information is saved on the app’s hosting server so reusable rooms can work. Ending a room prevents people from joining it, but some saved room records may remain in server storage. Active-room alerts are removed during normal room updates after their display period. The hosting provider processes data needed to run the server.

Use a name you are comfortable sharing with everyone in your room. Anyone with the invite code or link can attempt to join, so share it privately.

Terms of use

By using WalkieTalkiePro, you agree to use it lawfully and respectfully. Do not use it to threaten, harass, impersonate, or share content you do not have permission to share. Do not rely on the app for emergencies or safety-critical messages.

Internet-based voice-calling services, including services offered without a calling fee, may be restricted or regulated in some countries. Check that your use is permitted where you are. WalkieTalkiePro may be unavailable where its use is restricted. Your mobile carrier or internet provider may charge for data.

Rooms are shared spaces: participants can hear your live audio and see your name and shared alerts. Keep room invites private. Any room participant can leave, and any participant may end a room for everyone.

The service depends on your device, browser, internet connection, and hosting infrastructure. Availability, audio delivery, and speech recognition can be interrupted or inaccurate. The service is provided as available and may change or be unavailable without notice, subject to rights that cannot be limited under applicable law.

Safe use

  • Never hold or operate your phone while driving. Set up the room before the trip, and have a passenger handle the app.
  • Keep your attention on the road. Do not depend on this app for emergency communication, crash response, navigation, or guaranteed contact.
  • Internet access is required. The app may not work when connectivity is weak or unavailable, and it does not connect to cellular radio networks or walkie-talkies.
  • Speech transcripts can be wrong or missing, especially with noise, accents, or languages your browser does not recognize well. Confirm important messages by voice.
